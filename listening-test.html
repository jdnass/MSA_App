Playback test

This two-minute test repeats one Arabic recording. Each option uses the same audio.

Additional setting

Start with Direct server file.

  1. Load the test, then tap Play in the audio controls.
  2. Lock your phone and let it play for about 10 seconds.
  3. Pause from the lock screen. Wait 10 seconds, then press Play.
  4. Wait another 10 seconds and listen for the recording. Reopen MoSA and record the result below.

Reports stay on this device until you copy and share them. This test does not attempt to restart a stalled player.
