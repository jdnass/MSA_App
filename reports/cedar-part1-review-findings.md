# Al-Kitaab Part 1 audio review

Scope: 790 distinct readings, including vocabulary, extended forms, verb families, and broken plurals.

All 790 MP3s received a blind audio-input review: the reviewer heard each file without seeing the expected Arabic. A separate target-aware audio-input review was also run. These are automated model judgments, not human listening or phonetic certification.

## Summary

- 76 recordings have possible unmarked endings in the blind comparison.
- 6 recordings have possible added speech or repetitions in the blind comparison.
- 108 blind reviews explicitly reported uncertainty.
- 161 recordings are in the combined review queue, including target-aware flags and the user's examples. Counts overlap and are not confirmed-error counts.
- All 790 blind review requests completed. The target-aware pass returned usable judgments for 782 clips and unavailable results for 8; all 8 were included in the blind pass.
- No app text or recordings were changed.
